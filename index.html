Research

Cohomological Stability of Moduli Spaces of Rational Curves

Advisor: Prof. Zhiyu Tian (Peking University)

In this research, we study the cohomological stability of a `nice component' of the space of rational curves of degree k on M, where M is a moduli space. We construct the moduli space via extensions of sheaves, based on Castravet's work. Then I analyze the unstable locus, identifying it as an e-secant variety, and establish a stratification of singularities. Currently, I am analyzing the space of rational curves intersecting the unstable locus and relating it to Sym^e(C) by birational maps and the Projective Bundle Formula to establish the stability. An Original Proof of the Schläfli Formula

Advisor: Prof. Xiang Ma (Peking University)

This research established an elementary proof of the Schläfli formula in \mathbb{E}^3. By utilizing a tetrahedral decomposition method, we reduce the problem from arbitrary convex polyhedra to the specific case of a tetrahedron. The proof for the tetrahedron is then established through analytic geometry and algebraic calculation. [PDF] Read my expository note here.
