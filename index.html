Research

Homological Stability of Moduli Spaces of Rational Curves

Advisor: Prof. Zhiyu Tian (Peking University)

This project investigates the cohomological stability of a "nice component" of the Hilbert schemes of rational curves on M, where M is the moduli space of stable rank-2 vector bundles. My contribution was to identify the unstable locus in the odd degree case as a highly singular k-secant variety, which poses a significant computational bottleneck. I am currently exploring methods to resolve this singularity.You can read my note here.

An Original Proof of the Schlaefli Formula

Advisor: Prof. Xiang Ma (Peking University)

As a freshman, I developed an original proof for the classic Schlaefli formula ($\sum l \frac{d\theta_l}{dt} = 0$). My demonstration utilized only elementary analytic geometry and properties of the adjugate matrix. You can read my expository note here.
